NEON CHAMBER

A shotgun is loaded with a random mix of LIVE and BLANK shells. You and the Rival Unit take turns choosing who to fire at — yourself, or them.

Blank on yourself — no damage, and you go again.
Live on yourself — you take damage, turn passes.
Firing at the rival — turn always passes after, live or not.

Items help you cheat the odds. First to zero HP loses.
